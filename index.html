Booking & Payment

A booking is confirmed only after the advance payment is received. Rates quoted per night cover the standard occupancy of up to 30 guests.

Check-in is at 11 AM and check-out by 11 AM the next day unless a different time slot is selected.

Farmhouse Rules

Aadhar proof of the booking guest is mandatory at the time of booking and check-in.

Smoking inside rooms, loud music after 10 PM, and events beyond the booked capacity are not permitted without prior approval.

Damages to property or missing items will be charged at replacement cost.

Liability & Safety

Swimming pool is used at the visitor's own risk. Children must always be accompanied by adults.

The farmhouse is not responsible for loss or damage to personal belongings.

Cancellation & Refunds

Privacy
